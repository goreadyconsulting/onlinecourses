In practice · Protect your access

Use the Authenticator app as configured. Keep your login and unique authentication QR code private, and lock your screen when you step away.

Student information

Keep it where it belongs.

Engine should remain the home for student data wherever possible. A request for a file does not automatically mean the file needs to leave.

Start with Engine

Use Engine for student information and the formal audit trail. Check whether the task can be completed there before creating another copy.

Share only what is needed

Confirm the recipient, the task and the minimum fields needed. Check attachments and who can access any shared file.

Use the approved secure method

If sharing student personal information is genuinely necessary, use INUNI’s approved secure/password-protected sharing method.

Keep student data out of personal email, personal drives and WhatsApp. Avoid unmanaged spreadsheets kept on your desktop.

A colleague’s request

They need application status for a task. Your workbook also includes passport details and notes they do not need.

Choose a way to handle it
Keep the task in the official system.If the colleague has the right access, use Engine to provide the information needed. This avoids creating another student-data copy.

A password alone does not make every transfer appropriate. The method, recipient and information all need to be right.

Access, devices & you

Protect the account. Protect the person.

Your work device and your own details both deserve care, whether you are at your desk, at home or travelling.

Access belongs to you

  • Use the Authenticator app as configured.
  • Never share login details or unique authentication QR codes.
  • Keep your access private when helping a colleague with theirs.

Work devices are for work

  • Keep your laptop with you or locked away. Never leave it unattended in public or visible in a car.
  • Do not let family members or other people use it.
  • Keep it updated and do not install unapproved software.

Protect your own information too

Be cautious with personal phone numbers, home addresses, travel plans and family details. A convincing contact could use these details to impersonate or defraud you.

AI & the official record

Useful AI. Carefully chosen inputs.

You can use AI responsibly. Personal or confidential information requires INUNI approval for that specific service and use.

01

Approve the use, not just the tool

Never paste student data, passport details, financial documents or confidential commercial information unless INUNI has approved that specific use.

02

Reduce what you give it

Prefer fictional, genuinely anonymised or non-sensitive information. Use only the minimum necessary information where appropriate.

03

Keep Engine as the audit trail

Check AI output before relying on it. Record important work on Engine; AI chats, local spreadsheets and messages do not replace the formal record.

If something goes wrong, report immediately.

A wrong recipient, lost device, suspicious email, exposed information or accidental sharing needs an immediate report through the approved incident reporting route. Take a safe containment step if available, without delaying the report.
